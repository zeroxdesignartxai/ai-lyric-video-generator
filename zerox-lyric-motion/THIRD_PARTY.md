Transformers.js downloads its ONNX runtime dependency (MIT) and model files on first use. Those files keep their upstream licenses and notices. Audio processing remains on the device. Users supply audio/images/GIFs under their own rights. No stock-asset subscription, proprietary effect API, or payment integration is used.
